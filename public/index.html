Up to four picks per match: goals, both teams to score, corners, result. Score = most likely scoreline that agrees with the goals picks.

Every prediction is logged before kickoff into an append-only ledger and committed to git; grading only adds the final score. A pick is made only when its market clears the threshold it earned in a walk-forward backtest (22 European divisions, 2019–2026). A best pick is either in its market's top-confidence tier (the model's most confident 15% of calls in that market, checked for calibration on past seasons) or a confident pick priced at least 2% above fair value at the best price found. Matches logged before 15 Oct used earlier rules (1X2 best pick at ). Leagues outside the top five and the Champions and Europa Leagues are no longer tracked; their earlier predictions stay in the ledger.

Backtests show this model does not beat bookmaker prices. Use this page to measure accuracy, not as betting advice.
